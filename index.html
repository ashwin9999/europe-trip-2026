Day-by-Day Itinerary

Sep 9 to Sep 21, 2026 — 13 days, 12 nights across Belgium, France & Switzerland

1
Sep 9
BrugesWed · 9 Sep✈️ Arrival · Big transit day
Fly Dublin → Amsterdam (Aer Lingus EI604, 06:15–08:55). A few hours' buffer through Haarlem, then the booked train run: Haarlem → Rotterdam Centraal (13:23) → Brussels-Midi → Bruges, arriving 17:26. Check in near the historic center.
EVENINGDrop bags, then a gentle first look: the Markt and the Belfry facade lit up for the evening, dinner nearby. Save Minnewater and the chocolate workshop for tomorrow.
Speculoos waffles with Belgian dark-chocolate sauce
2
Sep 10
BrugesThu · 10 Sep🥾 Dawn walk
EARLYDawn canal-ring walk (7 km, 7–8am), passing Minnewater (Lake of Love) and the Begijnhof — stone bridges, swans, empty streets and the best light of the day. The prettiest flat walk of the whole trip.
MORNINGBelfry climb at 9am (366 steps — go early to beat the queue). Then the Groeningemuseum for the Flemish Primitives (Van Eyck, Memling).
AFTERNOONCanal cruise (€15, 30 min — the classic Bruges view), then De Halve Maan brewery tour and rooftop pour, the only working brewery in the centre.
Buy Belgian chocolates from Dumon on Eiermarkt for the long train ahead
3
Sep 11
Bruges → LyonFri · 11 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Bruges. ~5 hrs total: local train Bruges → Brussels Midi (~1 hr), then a direct TGV Brussels Midi → Lyon Part-Dieu (no Paris transfer — book the seat reservation in advance). Check in near Croix-Rousse.
EVENINGSettle in with a first look at Vieux-Lyon, the UNESCO Renaissance old town — St-Jean Cathedral and cobbled lanes.
First bouchon dinner — quenelle, andouillette, the works. Lyon is France's food capital for a reason.
4
Sep 12
LyonSat · 12 Sep
MORNINGThe traboules — secret silk-workers' passageways threading through Vieux-Lyon and Croix-Rousse (free, self-guided). The one at 54 Rue Saint-Jean is the grandest.
AFTERNOONMusée des Beaux-Arts — one of France's finest collections after the Louvre.
EVENINGLes Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse — the legendary indoor food market for cheese, charcuterie and oysters.
Traboule at 54 Rue Saint-Jean — the most spectacular of them all
5
Sep 13
LyonSun · 13 Sep
MORNINGFunicular up Fourvière hill — the basilica, the free Roman amphitheatres, and the city's best panorama.
AFTERNOONThe Presqu'île peninsula — Place Bellecour, boutiques, and the futuristic Musée des Confluences where the two rivers meet.
Tarte praline — the bright-pink praline tart, a Lyon original
6
Sep 14
LyonMon · 14 Sep🍷 Day trip
Beaujolais wine country. ~45 min by train to Villefranche-sur-Saône; September is harvest season.
FULL DAYRolling vineyards, the golden-stone pierres dorées villages, and cellar tastings during the vendanges. Back by evening.
Pick up Bernachon pralines before heading to Annecy
7
Sep 15
Lyon → AnnecyTue · 15 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Lyon. Direct regional TER, Lyon Part-Dieu → Annecy (~2 hrs, no changes). Check in near the Vieille Ville.
AFTERNOONThe Vieille Ville — pastel arcaded houses over canal tributaries, the Palais de l'Isle (that triangular building mid-canal) and why they call it the "Venice of the Alps."
EVENINGJardins de l'Europe and the Pont des Amours at sunset, with the lake glowing.
Petits gâteaux de Savoie or a blueberry tarte from a local pâtisserie
8
Sep 16
AnnecyWed · 16 Sep🥾 Lake & trails
MORNINGSwim in Lac d'Annecy — one of Europe's cleanest lakes — off Plage des Marquisats or Plage d'Albigny (free), or rent a kayak/pedal boat (€15).
AFTERNOONCol de la Forclaz: bus up (~30 min, ~€4), then the loop hike (9.6 km, ~3.5–4 hrs) through alpine pastures with paragliders launching out over the turquoise water.
Crepes with Nutella and hazelnut, or tartiflette for dinner

Sep 17
AnnecyThu · 17 Sep
MORNINGAngon Waterfall trail (5 km, ~2–3 hrs) — a 35 m fall above the lake on a wild forest path, almost tourist-free.
AFTERNOONLakeside Promenade du Lac, last swim, and packing for the Swiss transit ahead.
Start near Angon village on the D909 — southeast shore of the lake
10
Sep 18
Annecy → InterlakenFri · 18 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Annecy. ~4-5.5 hrs: regional train Annecy → Geneva, then Swiss InterCity Geneva → Bern → Interlaken Ost. Very reliable Swiss rail — no real risk of missed connections. Check in near Höheweg.
EVENINGStretch your legs on the Höheweg promenade between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz — your first proper look at the Eiger–Mönch–Jungfrau wall.
Check jungfrau.ch webcams each morning here — save the marquee high-altitude day for the clearest skies
11
Sep 19
InterlakenSat · 19 Sep🥾 Ridge hike
MORNINGHarder Kulm — funicular up (CHF 34 return), then the ridge trail for the postcard panorama of both lakes and the full Eiger–Mönch–Jungfrau trio.
AFTERNOONDown to the turquoise shore of Lake Brienz at Bönigen — lakeside walk, maybe a quick (cold!) swim.
Worth checking the Berner Oberland / Jungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Half-Fare Card if you'll do 3+ paid mountain rides
12
Sep 20
InterlakenSun · 20 Sep⭐ Don't miss
Your last full day — save this for whatever the clearest weather window looks like.
FULL DAYOeschinensee day trip: train to Kandersteg (45 min), gondola up (CHF 28), then the Panoramaweg loop (10 km, ~4–5 hrs) high above a turquoise lake ringed by 3,500 m peaks.
Gentler option: stay lakeside and walk only the lower shore loop — pack tonight, early train tomorrow
13
Sep 21
Interlaken → Zürich → DublinMon · 21 Sep✈️ Check-out · Fly home
Check out Interlaken. Train Interlaken Ost → Bern → Zürich Flughafen (IC608 / IC707, 06:29–08:49). Fly Zürich (ZRH) → Dublin (DUB), 11:20–12:50 (direct, 2h30m).
MORNINGEarly train through the Bernese lowlands to the airport — grab breakfast at Zürich HB or airside before the flight.
12 nights, 3 countries, 4 stops — trimmed from the original 42-night route to fit an 8-working-day allowance. Safe travels home. 🇮🇪

Hiking Essentials to Pack

FootwearTrail running shoes or light hiking boots cover all hikes on this list. No mountaineering boots needed.
LayersSeptember mornings in the Alps (Interlaken) and pre-Alps (Annecy) can be 8–12°C at altitude — pack a mid-layer fleece.
WaterCarry 1.5L minimum for the Swiss hikes. Refill at mountain huts (free or €1) in Switzerland.
SnacksLäderach chocolate bars (Switzerland) and Bernachon pralines (Lyon) make outstanding trail snacks.
AppsDownload AllTrails and Komoot before leaving Ireland. Save offline maps for Switzerland and France.
Swiss PassesJungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Travel Pass gives discounts on gondolas and mountain trains — worth checking if doing 3+ paid rides.
WeatherIn the Alps, morning is usually clear. If clouds build by midday, that's normal — plan summit hikes for morning.
