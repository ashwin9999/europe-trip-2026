Day-by-Day Itinerary

Sep 9 to Sep 21, 2026 — 13 days, 12 nights across Belgium, France & Switzerland

1
Sep 9
BrugesWed · 9 Sep✈️ Arrival · Big transit day
Fly Dublin → Amsterdam (Aer Lingus EI604, 06:15–08:55). Quick stop in central Amsterdam, then FlixBus 826 — booked, Amsterdam Sloterdijk → Bruges Station (11:00–15:20, seat 11D, booking 339 226 3587). Replaces the old NS/Eurocity train chain, which was cancelled by the 9 September nationwide strike. Check in near the historic center.
MORNINGStraight from the airport into central Amsterdam — Van Stapele Koekmakerij (Rokin) for their famous chocolate-chip cookies, then a quick pass through the Bloemenmarkt. Hard cutoffs: leave Van Stapele by 9:45, leave Bloemenmarkt by 10:10, then taxi to Amsterdam Sloterdijk (Piarcoplein, Zone 1) — trams and metro aren't running that day — to board the 11:00 bus.
EVENINGDrop bags and check in, then the Evening Tour: The Dark Side of Bruges (20:00 slot, ~1.5 hrs) — off-the-beaten-track ghost stories and legends with a local guide, meeting by the statue on the Markt. Tour ends ~9:30pm at the Bauhaus bar with a free beer.
Landing in Bruges at 15:20 leaves a comfortable ~4.5 hr buffer before the 20:00 tour — plenty of room to rest and grab dinner first
Late dinner near the Markt after the tour, or a quick bite beforehand if check-in runs tight against the 20:00 start

Sep 10
BrugesThu · 10 Sep🥾 Dawn walk
EARLYDawn walk to Minnewater (Lake of Love) and the Begijnhof (7–8am) — stone bridges, swans, empty streets and the best light of the day, before the tour groups arrive.
MORNINGFood stops and wandering — Markt and Burg Square at a quiet pace, plus a chocolate stop before the tour.
AFTERNOON2-Hour Historical Walking Tour with Praline (2:00–4:00pm) — Markt, Burg, Belfry, Gruuthuyse, the canals, Dijver and Rozehoedkaai with a local guide, praline sample included.
EVENINGBath & Barley beer spa (7:00–8:00pm), then dinner nearby.
Buy Belgian chocolates from Dumon on Eiermarkt for the long train ahead

Sep 11
Bruges → LyonFri · 11 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Bruges. IC 510 Bruges → Brussels-Midi (10:58–11:53), then direct TGV 9830 Brussels-Midi → Lyon Part-Dieu (13:09–16:59, Coach 5 Seat 537, Ref 3EAHD4 — no Paris transfer). Only a 1h16m gap in Brussels — not enough for real sightseeing, just a coffee near the platform. Check in near Croix-Rousse.
EVENINGSettle in with a first look at Vieux-Lyon, the UNESCO Renaissance old town — St-Jean Cathedral and cobbled lanes.
First bouchon dinner — quenelle, andouillette, the works. Lyon is France's food capital for a reason.

Sep 12
LyonSat · 12 Sep🍷 Wine tour
MORNINGBig Traboules in Little Lyon with Konstantin — a guided walk through the secret silk-workers' passageways of Vieux-Lyon and Croix-Rousse, worth the guide fee for the non-public courtyards you can't access alone. The one at 54 Rue Saint-Jean is the grandest.
AFTERNOONBeaujolais wine tour — booked, 14:00, half day (~4 hrs). Rhône Trip via Viator, English-language, rolling vineyards and the golden-stone pierres dorées villages during harvest season.
EVENINGBack in Lyon by ~18:00 — Les Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse, the city's legendary indoor food hall, for cheese, charcuterie, oysters and a proper dinner among the stalls.
Traboule at 54 Rue Saint-Jean — the most spectacular of them all

LyonSun · 13 Sep
MORNINGFunicular up Fourvière hill — the basilica, the free Roman amphitheatres, and the city's best panorama.
AFTERNOONThe Presqu'île peninsula — Place Bellecour, then Rue de la République, Lyon's main shopping street, for boutiques and a browse. Pharmacy stop at Grande Pharmacie Lyonnaise (22 Rue de la République) while you're on that street. Finish with a riverside wander where the Rhône and Saône meet.
Tarte praline — the bright-pink praline tart, a Lyon original

LyonMon · 14 Sep

Pick up Bernachon pralines before heading to Annecy
Two shopping pockets across the Lyon leg: Rue de la République (Presqu'île, mainstream boutiques) on Sep 13, Croix-Rousse (independent, local) today

Lyon → AnnecyTue · 15 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Lyon. Direct regional TER, Lyon Part-Dieu → Annecy (~2 hrs, no changes). Check in near the Vieille Ville.
AFTERNOONThe Vieille Ville — pastel arcaded houses over canal tributaries, the Palais de l'Isle (that triangular building mid-canal) and why they call it the "Venice of the Alps."
EVENINGJardins de l'Europe and the Pont des Amours at sunset, with the lake glowing.
Petits gâteaux de Savoie or a blueberry tarte from a local pâtisserie

AnnecyWed · 16 Sep🥾 Lake & trails
MORNINGRent a kayak or pedal boat on Lac d'Annecy (€15) — one of Europe's cleanest lakes, from Plage des Marquisats or Plage d'Albigny, no swimming required to enjoy the water.
AFTERNOONCol de la Forclaz: bus up (~30 min, ~€4), then the loop hike (9.6 km, ~3.5–4 hrs) through alpine pastures with paragliders launching out over the turquoise water.
Crepes with Nutella and hazelnut, or tartiflette for dinner
Worth checking a bike rental for the Tour du Lac d'Annecy cycle path if a free morning opens up — mostly separated lakeside path with mountain views the whole way

Annecy → InterlakenFri · 18 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Annecy. ~4-5.5 hrs: regional train Annecy → Geneva, then Swiss InterCity Geneva → Bern → Interlaken Ost. Very reliable Swiss rail — no real risk of missed connections. Check in near Höheweg.

Sep 19
InterlakenSat · 19 Sep🥾 Valley & villages
MORNINGLauterbrunnen valley — the valley floor beneath sheer cliffs and waterfalls, then Trümmelbach Falls, glacier-fed falls thundering inside the mountain.
AFTERNOONCable car up to Mürren or Gimmelwald — car-free villages on the valley's west wall, quieter than the Grindelwald side, with the Eiger's north face straight across.
Worth checking the Berner Oberland / Jungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Half-Fare Card if you'll do 3+ paid mountain rides

InterlakenSun · 20 Sep⭐ Don't miss
Your last full day and the priority hike — save this for whatever the clearest weather window looks like.
FULL DAYBachalpsee via Grindelwald First — gondola up to First, then the trail out to Bachalpsee, a turquoise alpine lake mirroring the surrounding peaks. The signature hike of the whole Switzerland leg.
Gentler/rainy-day option: boat out to St. Beatus Caves on Lake Thun instead — pack tonight either way, early train tomorrow
Jungfraujoch and Oeschinensee were both deliberately skipped this trip — solo cost didn't justify it and it's a strong candidate for a future Switzerland trip

Hiking Essentials to Pack

FootwearTrail running shoes or light hiking boots cover all hikes on this list. No mountaineering boots needed.
LayersSeptember mornings in the Alps (Interlaken) and pre-Alps (Annecy) can be 8–12°C at altitude — pack a mid-layer fleece.
WaterCarry 1.5L minimum for the Swiss hikes. Refill at mountain huts (free or €1) in Switzerland.
SnacksLäderach chocolate bars (Switzerland) and Bernachon pralines (Lyon) make outstanding trail snacks.
AppsDownload AllTrails and Komoot before leaving Ireland. Save offline maps for Switzerland and France.
Swiss PassesJungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Travel Pass gives discounts on gondolas and mountain trains — worth checking if doing 3+ paid rides.
WeatherIn the Alps, morning is usually clear. If clouds build by midday, that's normal — plan summit hikes for morning.
