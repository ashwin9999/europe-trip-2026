Europe Solo Trip 2026

Sep 1 – Oct 13, 2026  ·  42 nights  

🇳🇱 Netherlands 🇧🇪 Belgium 🇫🇷 France 🇨🇭 Switzerland 🇮🇹 Italy

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Sep 1 to Oct 13, 2026 — 43 days, 42 nights across Europe

1
Sep 1
AmsterdamTue · 1 Sep✈️ Arrival · Check-in
Arrive Dublin → Amsterdam (Aer Lingus direct, ~1h35). Train Schiphol → Centraal (15 min, €5). Check in (Jordaan or De Pijp), drop bags.
AFTERNOONEase in with a slow Jordaan canal walk — Prinsengracht and Brouwersgracht (often called the city's prettiest canal). Coffee + warm appeltaart at Winkel 43.
EVENINGGolden-hour stroll along the canal belt, then your first Indonesian rijsttafel dinner — a Dutch institution.
Brouwersgracht at sunset — houseboats and 17th-century warehouses lit gold
Blauw (Amstelveenseweg) — best vegetarian rijsttafel in the city. Book ahead.
2
Sep 2
AmsterdamWed · 2 Sep
MORNINGGet to Albert Cuyp Market (De Pijp) early for stroopwafels hot off the iron. Then the Rijksmuseum — pre-book the 9am first slot for Rembrandt's Night Watch and Vermeer (2–3 hrs).
AFTERNOONDecompress in Vondelpark; coffee and people-watching in De Pijp.
EVENINGSunset canal cruise — book a small electric open boat, not the big glass-topped tourist barges (~€25).
Buy Rijksmuseum & Van Gogh tickets days ahead — timed slots sell out
Dessert: Patisserie Holtkamp — legendary Dutch pastries since 1969
3
Sep 3
AmsterdamThu · 3 Sep
MORNINGVan Gogh Museum (pre-booked 9am slot) — the world's largest Van Gogh collection, walked chronologically.
AFTERNOONJordaan deep-dive: hunt the hofjes (hidden almshouse courtyards), browse free independent galleries and the antique shops along Elandsgracht.
EVENINGPull up at a bruin café (brown bar) — Café Chris or 't Smalle — for a canalside Dutch beer.
Begijnhof — a secret 14th-c courtyard garden in the city centre (free), home to Amsterdam's oldest wooden house
4
Sep 4
AmsterdamFri · 4 Sep🚆 Day trip
Day trip — Zaanse Schans. Train Centraal → Zaandijk Zaanse Schans (17 min, €3.70 each way), then a 15-min walk. Half a day is plenty.
MORNINGWorking windmills on the river, plus clog-carving and cheese-making workshops in a preserved Dutch village.
EVENINGBack in town, take the free ferry behind Centraal to NDSM Wharf for street art and an industrial-waterfront sunset.
Lanskroon bakery — stroopwafels made fresh daily
5
Sep 5
AmsterdamSat · 5 Sep
MORNINGFree ferry across the IJ to the EYE Film Museum — striking architecture, free ground floor and café with a skyline view.
AFTERNOONIf you snagged tickets, the Anne Frank House (deeply moving); otherwise Het Scheepvaartmuseum (maritime) or one last canal-belt wander + souvenir shopping.
EVENINGFarewell Amsterdam dinner; pack for Haarlem.
Anne Frank House sells out: tickets go online only, released ~6 weeks before — set a reminder
6
Sep 6
HaarlemSun · 6 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Amsterdam. Train Amsterdam → Haarlem (20 min, €4.50, several per hour). Check in near Grote Markt. Haarlem is Amsterdam's quieter, prettier cousin — same Golden-Age looks, none of the crowds.
AFTERNOONThe Grote Markt — widely called the most beautiful square in the Netherlands. Wander the Gouden Straatjes (golden little streets) of independent boutiques.
EVENINGDinner on a Grote Markt terrace; riverside walk along the Spaarne.
Haarlem stroopwafel shops — the city that claims to have invented them
7
Sep 7
HaarlemMon · 7 Sep🏖️ Beach
Haarlem's museums close on Mondays — so today is for the market and the sea.
MORNINGMonday market on Grote Markt (voted the country's best) — flowers, cheese, herring. Pop into St-Bavokerk for the giant Müller organ that Mozart and Handel both played (open Mon–Sat).
AFTERNOONBus 81 to Bloemendaal aan Zee (under 10 km) — wide North Sea beach backed by dunes. Walk the sand, swim if it's mild, beach-bar drink.
Windmill De Adriaan on the Spaarne — small museum, big riverside photo
8
Sep 8
HaarlemTue · 8 Sep
MORNINGFrans Hals Museum — the world's largest collection of Hals' portraits, split over two sites a 7-min walk apart.
AFTERNOONTeylers Museum — the oldest museum in the Netherlands (1778): fossils, minerals, scientific instruments and Old Master drawings in a candle-era interior.
EVENINGSlow Spaarne-side dinner. Last stroopwafel run before Belgium.
Teylers' Oval Room — lit by daylight only, exactly as it was 240 years ago
9
Sep 9
BrugesWed · 9 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Haarlem. Train to Bruges (~3 hrs — Haarlem → Rotterdam Centraal, transfer to an Intercity toward Antwerp, then direct into Bruges; 1-2 changes, check NS/SNCB timetables closer to travel). Check in near the historic center.
AFTERNOONStart gently: Minnewater (Lake of Love) and the Begijnhof — a white-walled courtyard of former lay-sisters' houses. Drift up to the Markt for the Belfry's facade.
EVENINGA chocolate workshop or tasting at The Chocolate Line or Olivier Willems for hazelnut pralines.
Speculoos waffles with Belgian dark-chocolate sauce
10
Sep 10
BrugesThu · 10 Sep🥾 Dawn walk
EARLYDawn canal-ring walk (7 km, 7–8am) — stone bridges, swans, empty streets and the best light of the day. The prettiest flat walk of the whole trip.
MORNINGBelfry climb at 9am (366 steps — go early to beat the queue). Then the Groeningemuseum for the Flemish Primitives (Van Eyck, Memling).
AFTERNOONCanal cruise (€15, 30 min — the classic Bruges view), then De Halve Maan brewery tour and rooftop pour, the only working brewery in the centre.
Buy Belgian chocolates from Dumon on Eiermarkt for the long train ahead
11
Sep 11
Bruges → LyonFri · 11 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Bruges. ~5 hrs total: local train Bruges → Brussels Midi (~1 hr), then a direct TGV Brussels Midi → Lyon Part-Dieu (no Paris transfer — book the seat reservation in advance). Check in near Croix-Rousse.
EVENINGSettle in with a first look at Vieux-Lyon, the UNESCO Renaissance old town — St-Jean Cathedral and cobbled lanes.
First bouchon dinner — quenelle, andouillette, the works. Lyon is France's food capital for a reason.
12
Sep 12
LyonSat · 12 Sep
MORNINGThe traboules — secret silk-workers' passageways threading through Vieux-Lyon and Croix-Rousse (free, self-guided). The one at 54 Rue Saint-Jean is the grandest.
AFTERNOONMusée des Beaux-Arts — one of France's finest collections after the Louvre.
EVENINGLes Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse — the legendary indoor food market for cheese, charcuterie and oysters.
Traboule at 54 Rue Saint-Jean — the most spectacular of them all
13
Sep 13
LyonSun · 13 Sep
MORNINGFunicular up Fourvière hill — the basilica, the free Roman amphitheatres, and the city's best panorama.
AFTERNOONThe Presqu'île peninsula — Place Bellecour, boutiques, and the futuristic Musée des Confluences where the two rivers meet.
Tarte praline — the bright-pink praline tart, a Lyon original
14
Sep 14
LyonMon · 14 Sep🍷 Day trip
Beaujolais wine country. ~45 min by train to Villefranche-sur-Saône; September is harvest season.
FULL DAYRolling vineyards, the golden-stone pierres dorées villages, and cellar tastings during the vendanges. Back by evening.
Pick up Bernachon pralines before heading to Annecy
15
Sep 15
Lyon → AnnecyTue · 15 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Lyon. Direct regional TER, Lyon Part-Dieu → Annecy (~2 hrs, no changes). Check in near the Vieille Ville.
AFTERNOONThe Vieille Ville — pastel arcaded houses over canal tributaries, the Palais de l'Isle (that triangular building mid-canal) and why they call it the "Venice of the Alps."
EVENINGJardins de l'Europe and the Pont des Amours at sunset, with the lake glowing.
Petits gâteaux de Savoie or a blueberry tarte from a local pâtisserie
16
Sep 16
AnnecyWed · 16 Sep🥾 Lake & trails
MORNINGSwim in Lac d'Annecy — one of Europe's cleanest lakes — off Plage des Marquisats or Plage d'Albigny (free), or rent a kayak/pedal boat (€15).
AFTERNOONCol de la Forclaz: bus up (~30 min, ~€4), then the loop hike (9.6 km, ~3.5–4 hrs) through alpine pastures with paragliders launching out over the turquoise water.
Crepes with Nutella and hazelnut, or tartiflette for dinner
17
Sep 17
AnnecyThu · 17 Sep
MORNINGAngon Waterfall trail (5 km, ~2–3 hrs) — a 35 m fall above the lake on a wild forest path, almost tourist-free.
AFTERNOONLakeside Promenade du Lac, last swim, and packing for the long Swiss transit ahead.
Start near Angon village on the D909 — southeast shore of the lake
18
Sep 18
Annecy → InterlakenFri · 18 Sep🚆 Big transit day
Check out Annecy. ~4-5.5 hrs: regional train Annecy → Geneva, then Swiss InterCity Geneva → Bern → Interlaken Ost. Very reliable Swiss rail — no real risk of missed connections. Check in near Höheweg.
EVENINGStretch your legs on the Höheweg promenade between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz — your first proper look at the Eiger–Mönch–Jungfrau wall.
Check jungfrau.ch webcams each morning here — save the marquee high-altitude days for the clearest skies
19
Sep 19
InterlakenSat · 19 Sep🥾 Ridge hike
MORNINGHarder Kulm — funicular up (CHF 34 return), then the ridge trail for the postcard panorama of both lakes and the full Eiger–Mönch–Jungfrau trio.
AFTERNOONDown to the turquoise shore of Lake Brienz at Bönigen — lakeside walk, maybe a quick (cold!) swim.
Worth checking the Berner Oberland / Jungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Half-Fare Card if you'll do 3+ paid mountain rides
20
Sep 20
InterlakenSun · 20 Sep⭐ Don't miss
Save this for a clear day — swap with tomorrow if the morning is cloudy.
FULL DAYOeschinensee day trip: train to Kandersteg (45 min), gondola up (CHF 28), then the Panoramaweg loop (10 km, ~4–5 hrs) high above a turquoise lake ringed by 3,500 m peaks.
Gentler option: stay lakeside and walk only the lower shore loop
21
Sep 21
InterlakenMon · 21 Sep
MORNINGBoat across Lake Brienz to Giessbach Falls (ride the world's oldest funicular), then a gentle lakeshore walk.
AFTERNOONSt. Beatus Caves on Lake Thun (bus 21, 10 min) — a dramatic cave system behind a waterfall. A relaxed half-day before Grindelwald.
Rainy plan: Thun day trip (30 min), Funky Chocolate Club workshop, or the Bödelibad thermal pool & sauna
22
Sep 22
GrindelwaldTue · 22 Sep🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Interlaken. Scenic Berner Oberland Bahn (BOB) mountain train, Interlaken Ost → Grindelwald (~30-35 min, short and scenic, no issues). Check in in the village.
AFTERNOONFirst gondola up (25 min) to the First Cliff Walk by Tissot — a steel walkway and suspension bridge cantilevered over the void, staring straight at the Eiger North Face (free once you're at First).
OPTIONALAdrenaline add-ons at First: the First Flyer zipline (800 m, up to 83 km/h) or the First Glider.
Bäckerei Fuchs — handmade Grindelwald hazelnut pralines, apple strudel at mountain huts
23
Sep 23
GrindelwaldWed · 23 Sep⭐ Top of Europe
The trip's headline day — book ahead, and do it on your clearest Grindelwald morning (check the webcams at breakfast).
FULL DAYJungfraujoch via the Eiger Express to 3,454 m: the Aletsch Glacier (longest in the Alps), the Ice Palace carved into the glacier, and the Sphinx observation deck. ~CHF 220–260 return (free with Jungfrau Travel Pass, 50% off with Half-Fare Card).
Reserve the cog-train section (Eigergletscher → Jungfraujoch) in peak season — slots fill
24
Sep 24
GrindelwaldThu · 24 Sep🥾 Panoramic walk
MORNINGMännlichen → Kleine Scheidegg panoramic walk — gondola up to Männlichen, then an easy ridge trail (~2 hrs) face-to-face with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau the whole way.
AFTERNOONTrain down from Kleine Scheidegg; slow village time and packing for the long haul to Italy.
Optional detour: Bachalpsee lake from First (6 km round trip) if you have energy left — mirror reflections of the Schreckhorn & Wetterhorn
25
Sep 25
Grindelwald → BolzanoFri · 25 Sep🚆 Longest transit day
Check out Grindelwald. ~6-6.5 hrs — the longest travel day of the trip: mountain train down to Interlaken Ost, EuroCity via Milan Central, then a fast train Milan → Bolzano. Plan this as a low-key arrival day. Check in near Piazza Walther (Old Town).
EVENINGGentle first look: Piazza Walther, the elegant main square, and a wander through the medieval arcades of Via dei Portici.
Austro-Italian pastries and a South Tyrolean dinner — the food here is a unique German-Italian mashup
26
Sep 26
BolzanoSat · 26 Sep🥾 Cable car & hike
MORNINGRenon Cable Car (Via Renon 12, ~€15 return) up to the Ritten/Renon plateau, then an easy 30-min hike to the Earth Pyramids — Europe's tallest, most perfectly-shaped examples, some 25,000 years old, with Dolomites views the whole way.
AFTERNOONSouth Tyrol Museum of Archaeology — home to Ötzi the Iceman, Europe's oldest natural mummy (~€13).
The open-air market on Via Argentieri — fresh fruit, wild herbs, cured meats, still-warm loaves
27
Sep 27
BolzanoSun · 27 Sep
MORNINGCastel Roncolo (Runkelstein Castle) — the best-preserved medieval fresco cycle north of the Alps (~€8).
AFTERNOONMuseion contemporary art museum, or the South Tyrol Museum of Natural History for Dolomites geology; last old-town wander before Lake Garda.
Speck, Knödel (dumplings), and a glass of Lagrein — classic South Tyrolean dinner
28
Sep 28
Bolzano → Riva del GardaMon · 28 Sep🚆🚌 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Bolzano. ~1.5-2 hrs: train Bolzano → Rovereto (~1 hr on the main Brenner line), then Bus 332 direct to Riva del Garda (~45 min, can stretch to 1 hr with traffic — check the Trentino Trasporti timetable a few days ahead, don't wing it). Check in near the coast, close to Piazza III Novembre.
AFTERNOONTorre Apponale and the MAG museum; funicular up to the Bastione for a first lake panorama.
EVENINGLakefront aperitivo at Piazza III Novembre as the light fades over the water.
Wood-fired Margherita pizza and truffle pasta — Trentino specialities
29
Sep 29
Lake Garda (Riva)Tue · 29 Sep🥾 Viewpoint hike
MORNINGPunta Larici hike (5.3 km, ~2 hrs, 330 m elevation gain) — medium-difficulty climb to a spectacular clifftop viewpoint over the whole lake.
AFTERNOONWind down at the Bastione or a lakeside beach; easy evening.
Alternative/extra: Monte Brione or the Chapel of Santa Barbara for shorter lake views
30
Sep 30
Lake Garda (Riva)Wed · 30 Sep⛴️ Ferry day
MORNINGStrada del Ponale — a historic road turned coastal hiking/biking path, starting right at the Riva ferry stop; the elevated Ciclopista del Garda juts ~50 m above the lake, one of the most spectacular stretches on all of Lake Garda.
AFTERNOONFerry to Limone sul Garda (20–45 min, €5–7 one-way, roughly every 3 hrs) — lemon groves and lakefront wandering.
Limone's famous lemon gelato and limoncello
31
Oct 1
Riva del Garda → VeronaThu · 1 Oct🚌🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Riva del Garda. ~1.5-2 hrs: Bus 332 back to Rovereto (~45 min–1 hr), then a direct train Rovereto → Verona Porta Nuova (~40 min). This order (Garda before Verona) avoids backtracking through Rovereto twice. Check in near Piazza delle Erbe.
AFTERNOONThe Roman Arena and Piazza Brà — smaller than the Colosseum but older, and still an active theatre.
EVENINGAperol spritz on Piazza delle Erbe, Verona's oldest square, built atop the old Roman forum.
First proper gelato stop of the Italian leg — nocciola, gianduja
32
Oct 2
VeronaFri · 2 Oct
MORNINGClimb Torre dei Lamberti for the best rooftop view in the city, then Juliet's House and the Scaliger Tombs.
AFTERNOONWalk across Ponte Pietra, the oldest Roman bridge in the city.
EVENINGSunset at Castel San Pietro — the classic postcard view over Verona and the Adige River.
Opera at the Arena runs most Fri/Sat/Sun nights May–Aug (up to 6 nights/week in peak summer) — check if anything's on
33
Oct 3
Verona → FlorenceSat · 3 Oct🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Verona. Direct Frecciarossa, Verona Porta Nuova → Firenze Santa Maria Novella (~1.5 hrs, no changes). Check in in the Oltrarno.
EVENINGWalk across the Ponte Vecchio, then up to Piazzale Michelangelo for the classic sunset over the Duomo and the Arno.
First gelato: Gelateria dei Neri — hazelnut and dark chocolate, mandatory
Book NOW if you haven't: Uffizi, Accademia/David, Duomo dome climb (Brunelleschi Pass)
34
Oct 4
FlorenceSun · 4 Oct⭐ Uffizi & Duomo
MORNINGUffizi Gallery (pre-booked first slot) — Botticelli's Birth of Venus, Leonardo, Caravaggio (3–4 hrs).
AFTERNOONDuomo complex — climb Brunelleschi's Dome (timed slot), Giotto's Campanile and the Baptistery.
EVENINGDinner upstairs at the Mercato Centrale food hall.
Cantucci dipped in Vin Santo to finish dinner
35
Oct 5
FlorenceMon · 5 Oct🚆 Day trip
Lucca day trip. ~20 min direct train from Santa Maria Novella.
FULL DAYThe walled city of Lucca — walk (or cycle) the flat pedestrian loop atop the intact Renaissance walls, then wander the old town's church-dotted lanes. Far more relaxed pace than Florence.
Rent a bike right at the wall gate for a fast, breezy full loop
36
Oct 6
Florence → Cinque TerreTue · 6 Oct🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Florence. Train Florence → Pisa, change for La Spezia, then the local Cinque Terre Express to Monterosso (~2.5 hrs total, stops at all 5 villages every ~20-30 min). Check in in Monterosso. Buy the Cinque Terre Card (3-day, covers trails + unlimited local trains).
AFTERNOONSwim off the rocks or on Monterosso's sandy beach — the only real beach of the five villages.
EVENINGTrofie al pesto and focaccia at the harbour as the light goes — pesto was born here.
Limoncino semifreddo — a lemon-cream dessert unique to this coast
37
Oct 7
Cinque TerreWed · 7 Oct⭐ Blue Trail
EARLYSentiero Azzurro (Blue Trail) — start 7am from Monterosso → Vernazza (the best and most dramatic section, above terraced vineyards and the sea).
EVENINGSunset at Manarola's Punta Bonfiglio with a bottle of local Sciacchetrà.
Aperitivo at Nessun Dorma, Manarola — reserve or arrive early, it fills fast
38
Oct 8
Cinque TerreThu · 8 Oct
MORNINGSlow village morning — Vernazza's castle ruins and harbour (the most photogenic of the five), then clifftop Corniglia for a quiet Vermentino tasting.
AFTERNOONA final long swim, or the wilder, near-empty Monterosso → Levanto trail (5 km, free).
Buy a jar of Monterosso pesto to bring home before you leave
39
Oct 9
Cinque Terre → NiceFri · 9 Oct🚆 Check-out → Check-in
Check out Cinque Terre. Scenic coastal train La Spezia → Genoa, change to a regional train along the coast to Ventimiglia (Italy/France border), transfer directly onto a French TER into Nice Ville (~4.5 hrs total — trains are timed to connect, no long border wait). Check in in Vieux Nice.
EVENINGFirst stroll through Vieux Nice and along the Promenade des Anglais.
Socca — Nice's chickpea-flour street food classic
40
Oct 10
NiceSat · 10 Oct
MORNINGCours Saleya flower market — Nice's most famous market, flowers, produce, and Provençal soaps.
AFTERNOONCastle Hill for the best panoramic view over the Baie des Anges.
EVENINGDinner in the old town's narrow lanes.
Castle Hill at sunrise, if you can manage an early start — near-empty and spectacular
41
Oct 11
NiceSun · 11 Oct🚆 Day trip
Menton. ~40 min direct train from Nice Ville, frequent departures.
FULL DAYOne of the most colourful Riviera towns — the Promenade de la Mer up to Port de Garavan, the fragrant Val Rahmeh Botanical Garden, and (if energetic) the Sentier littoral du Cap Martin past Eileen Gray's Villa E-1027.
Menton lemon tart — the town's signature citrus dessert
42
Oct 12
NiceMon · 12 Oct🥾 Coastal walk
Cap d'Antibes. ~20 min direct train from Nice (~€4).
FULL DAYSentier du Littoral (Sentier de Tirepoil) — a narrow 1–2 hr coastal trail hugging the cliffs of Cap d'Antibes, a quieter alternative to Monaco or Èze. Grippy shoes recommended, swimmers in summer.
Bring at least 1L of water and sun protection — minimal shade on the trail
43
Oct 13
DepartureTue · 13 Oct✈️ Check-out · Fly home
Check out Nice. Local tram from central Nice to Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE), ~30 min. Fly Ryanair FR3955, Nice (NCE) → Dublin (DUB), 10:45–12:25.
MORNINGLast coffee and croissant in Vieux Nice.
AFTERNOONDepart for the airport with plenty of buffer.
42 nights, 5 countries, 13 stops — buongiorno to goodnight. Safe travels home. 🇮🇪

Hiking Essentials to Pack

FootwearTrail running shoes or light hiking boots cover all hikes on this list. No mountaineering boots needed.
LayersSeptember mornings in the Alps (Grindelwald, Interlaken, Annecy) can be 8–12°C at altitude — pack a mid-layer fleece.
WaterCarry 1.5L minimum for Swiss and Italian hikes. Refill at mountain huts (free or €1) in Switzerland.
SnacksLäderach chocolate bars (Switzerland) and Ligurian focaccia (Cinque Terre) make outstanding trail snacks.
AppsDownload AllTrails and Komoot before leaving Ireland. Save offline maps for Switzerland and Cinque Terre trails.
Swiss PassesJungfrau Travel Pass or Swiss Travel Pass gives discounts on gondolas and mountain trains — worth checking if doing 3+ paid rides.
WeatherIn the Alps, morning is usually clear. If clouds build by midday, that's normal — plan summit hikes for morning.
Trail StatusCheck Cinque Terre National Park website (parconazionale5terre.it) for trail closures before you go — sections close after rain.
